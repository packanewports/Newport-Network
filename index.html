About Packa

The coolest shitposter on the internet

My name is Packa, or Packanewports, I am 16 years old and I run the coolest shitpost account on twitter, along with this website. I have a lot of hobbies and interests, I consider myself a jack of all trades type, "jack of all trades, master of none, but oftentimes better than master of one" fits me far too well. I do a lot of things, but im not the best at any of them, in the end I do it all for fun so its ok. To list things that I do, I make music online and in my band, I'm a mediocore artist, I'm a coder (obviously), I make video games, "ethical" hacking, I do pottery, I make movies and plays, I'm an amateur photographer (look forward to that blog), I skateboard, I play all forms of percussion but mainly the drumset, I play piano, guitar, and banjo, I love to read but mainly political theory, philosophy, and most other forms of classical and contemporary lierature, I love to write as well, I could probably go on forever but I think you get the idea. My name comes from my favorite cig obviously, I've always been a fan of newports, and I felt like the name was cute and fitting. I take part in most illicit things you can think of, on occassions the extreme, not to be some edgelord ofc. I love all my oomfs and I love meeting new people, you guys are the best and I couldn't be happier! If you have any questions about me, feel free to ask me anywhere or anytime!

About this site

What is this site?

This site is a personal website for me, Packa, to share my thoughts and introduce myself. I could've used a strawpage, but I'm simply better. This site will come to include a blog, somewhere I can post my ideas and feelings when twitters dry, it will also include an art museum, similair to straw pages anonymous drawing system you can draw something and frame it on the wall, an anon wall, where you can write whatever you want and use it like your own little blog, and a few other things soon to come!
